What the result means

  • Ready: choose the order on Import orders, then select or create a roast plan.
  • Dismissed: restore it explicitly before it can enter production.
  • Already in a plan: open that plan; do not add a duplicate.
  • Closed plan: view its read-only completed record. Reopen only when a correction is needed.
  • Needs review: confirm instructions, coffee choices, bag sizes, grind or changed source items.
  • Paid / fulfillment hold: recovery keeps Shopify’s status. Cancelled, unpaid, partial or scheduled deliveries do not become ready just because they were recovered.
  • Not found: check the number and connected store. Older orders may require a Shopify orders CSV because this connection normally has recent-order access.
