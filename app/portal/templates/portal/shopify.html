Set up from your signed-in laptop

  1. Sign in to this portal as the employee or the pay manager on the laptop where you can access Shopify.
  2. In Shopify’s Dev Dashboard, create an app for this store. Set the app URL to your configured HTTPS address/shopify/, turn off embedding, and allow this redirect URL: {{ callback }}.
  3. Give the app read_orders and read_products, then release/install it on the store and approve its permissions. Customer names are optional and may require protected customer data approval. No addresses or emails are needed for roasting.
  4. Enter the store’s original myshopify.com address, app client ID and client secret below. Save, then authorize the connection. Approve Shopify’s permission screen on your laptop.

If the app and store belong to the same Shopify organization, the same-organization button can connect the installed app directly. Otherwise use the authorization button.

Orders available through read_orders cover the recent 60-day window. Older history stays available through CSV exports. A refresh loads up to 100 orders; use it again if more remain.

Subscriptions: your customers pay on the recurrence date. Each successful renewal creates its own order, which appears here with its real Shopify order ID. Upcoming forecasts still use your Shopify Subscriptions export; this new app cannot assume access to contracts owned by Shopify’s subscription app.
