2. Set up the sheet

US Letter, 8.5 × 11 inches. Four-per-sheet uses two columns and two rows; eight-per-sheet uses two columns and four rows. Positions run left to right, then top to bottom.

Sheet dimensions and alignment

The four-label layout leaves 0.0625-inch outer margins and 0.0625-inch cutting gaps. The complete artwork fills each label as closely as its proportions allow, without cropping or stretching. Original artwork colors are preserved; the surrounding sheet and any padding are black. The alignment test stays white to save ink. Print it on plain paper before using your label stock.

3. Preview, download, and print

Print at 100% / Actual size on Letter paper. Turn off “Fit to page” and use a plain-paper alignment test first. PDF pages contain the selected labels plus the advance labels shown above. Any positions that cannot be matched stay blank.

Artwork is preserved as supplied. When an artwork has mapped grind boxes, Crema marks Whole Bean or Ground on order labels. Mark unmapped or spare labels and roast dates by hand. Regular, Fine, and Coarse instructions remain on the packing list.

Extra-label queue

Mark a sheet printed once it is printed. Release a reservation if unused; discard printed copies before releasing them. When using saved extras, reduce the Labels to print count above.
