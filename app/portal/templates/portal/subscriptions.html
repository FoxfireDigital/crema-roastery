Can a read scope keep this forecast updated?

Shopify’s read_own_subscription_contracts scope covers contracts created and managed by our app. Your existing contracts belong to the Shopify Subscriptions app, so adding this scope would not make their schedules available here. Product access and order access do not include those contracts.

The export below remains the forecast source. Each paid renewal can already be refreshed through Import orders. A merchant-configured Shopify Flow export is a separate integration to investigate; it is not connected to this portal.
