Daily routine

Receive or import real orders, resolve item decisions, weigh available roasted stock, review suggested loads and accept a plan. Record actual weights on Today; then pack using the separate customer instructions. Close completed plans after checks pass.

Set up your roastery

In Administration add active roasters with their safe green-input capacity. Add coffees, kind and standard roast. Set a default Medium base for every flavor and exclude half-caff or decaf coffees from flavor-base use. Portal configuration sets your display name, fallback Medium base, four optional assortment coffees and Shopify Admin bookmark. Add users and deliberately grant staff status. PayrollAccess controls who can manage rates.

Orders, plans and batches

An order describes finished coffee and packaging. A plan combines compatible demand for a roasting session. A batch is one physical roaster load with measured green input and an actual finished result. Planning estimates are not actual measurements.

Import orders

Open Marketplaces & stores and choose the platform you use. Select several checkboxes for multiple marketplaces, or choose manual orders / CSV only. Save the choices, then add independently named stores. Only selected platforms appear in the connection form, with fields for that platform. Existing connections stay selected on upgrade; pause an active store before removing its platform. Removing a paused platform retains its orders, mappings and credentials. Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix and Squarespace adapters read orders; the new adapters are experimental until verified with a merchant account. Use the existing Shopify screen for a store already connected there. Never add it again under a new identity. Refreshing stages orders without creating a plan. Automatic checks are optional; Continue refresh resumes a saved position.

For other exports, use the general CSV importer: choose the owning store, preview the file and map stable order/line IDs, dates with timezones, payment and fulfillment status, title, quantity, size and grind. Net coffee weight must be per bag, never shipping weight. Preview uploads expire after 30 minutes. Resolve item decisions, then select orders for a plan. Unknown payment, partial fulfillment, cancellations and unsupported delivery states stay held. Dismiss hides an order across refreshes until restored. Identical refreshes retain deliberate assortments. Source changes send affected accepted plans back to review without changing recorded roast results.

Each charged renewal uses its actual source order ID. The portal does not take payment, ship, fulfill orders or assume future deliveries. Whole bean, Regular ground, Fine and Coarse are separate instructions. Credentials belong only in protected connection settings. See docs/MARKETPLACES.md for access requirements and limitations.

Subscriptions

Each charged renewal enters through its real Shopify order ID. General assortments are chosen and split for each cycle; portions must conserve the original source weight. Upcoming contracts use the subscription CSV; access to another app’s subscription contracts is not assumed. Forecast labels are not production orders.

Wholesale

Enter a customer and rows for each coffee, grind and bag size: 12 oz, 5 lb or 2.5 oz fracs. Optional four-coffee assortments require four different active coffees in Portal configuration. Save usual customer preferences separately from a real order.

Planning and stock credits

Suggestions use 5% extra finished coffee by default, then estimated roast loss to calculate green input. Reserve manually counted bulk whole beans first. Review reserved versus actually used bulk; only actual usage belongs in recorded output. Started groups do not automatically grow tiny loads when edited. Review genuine shortages and add a deliberate load if necessary. Custom single-origin requests require at least 80 finished ounces; blends and flavors stay standard. Experimental batches can use any roast with an origin note.

Record roasting

Today groups accepted loads by machine, then light to dark. Green input must fit machine capacity. Measured deducts the frozen green recipe once. You may mark Roasted before entering the result; a result also marks Roasted. Bagging and sealing follow separate packing checks. Keep failed saves visible, reconnect and retry; copy unsaved values before reloading.

Today has Measured, Roasted and Cooled checkboxes. Entering a finished weight automatically marks Roasted; mark Cooled yourself after cooling finishes. Hide cooled removes finished roasting rows from view without changing any record. Bagging, grinding, labeling and sealing are recorded on Bagging / labels. Today saves never clear those packing records. Use Review roast / logs to see attached foreground logs and their saved reference overlaid directly below Completed Artisan logs. Older attachments have page controls; originals remain downloadable.

Green inventory and suppliers

Green-stock checks are optional on each plan. Use Skip stock check to continue with availability marked not checked; configured measured deductions still apply. Shortages can request a different green origin, ranked by documented roast suitability first and shared flavor tags second. Approve only after checking caffeine type and product/label suitability. Approval applies a frozen ingredient recipe to this plan group's unmeasured loads, preserving global recipes and measured history. Changed counts or plans require a fresh request. Cancel affects only remaining loads; prepare a fresh Artisan folder after approval or cancellation. Add optional roast levels, flavor tags and caffeine type in each green origin's details.

Create each green origin or lot and enter actual counts. Blend recipes describe separate origins and total 100%. Add suppliers and origin details separately. Receive a purchase to add green stock once; enter optional landed cost and currency. Invoice edits and voiding details leave physical stock unchanged. Correct quantities with a fresh count. Missing costs stay unknown, and currencies are never combined.

Bulk roasted beans

Update actual bulk whole-bean counts before flavoring or grinding. Select which coffees appear using bulk_stock_enabled in Administration. Plans reserve compatible coffee, roast and temperature; recording use is separate from reserving. Counts are manual because work outside the portal can change stock.

Bagging and labels

On Today, open the collapsible card for a customer/order beneath the roast schedule. Flavoring, grinding, bagging, labeling and sealing save automatically to the same records used by Bagging & labels. Each accepted plan has its own completion count and Preview & download label sheets button. Full label requirements remain on Bagging & labels. Reload the other screen to see its latest saved state. If another person changed a checklist, reload before retrying; stale changes are never silently applied. Administrators can adjust grind and bag counts inside each card after clearing its checklist.

Packing retains the ordered flavor, grind, size, customer and special label, even when several products share a physical Medium roast. Upload your own PNG, JPEG or WebP artwork and confirm its metadata before marking it ready. Four labels fit a Letter sheet; fracs rotate onto eight horizontal spaces. Test on plain paper at actual size. Optional fillers use known next-14-day subscriptions, then matching-size bags ordered in the last 21 days. They fill only the last sheet and never create coffee orders.

Tasting notebook

Link notes to a roasted batch or origin sample. Optional house ratings use 0–10; blanks remain unscored. Complete all axes for a radar shape. Scores do not change yields or stock. Archive mistakes with a reason and keep edit history. Attached Artisan flavor attributes show their original values using a labelled display maximum; they are separate from portal tastings.

Artisan profiles and alarms

Upload your own Fahrenheit .alog originals. Preview, approve machines and roast, and set Charge and Drop targets. Derived alarm copies mark Charge, Drop and Off at Drop plus 0.1°F with event guards. The optional Windows helper checks for an open Artisan instance and asks for deliberate loading. Preparation titles use coffee name, scheduled green ounces to tenths, First=1 or Second=2, and C/C+/FC/FC+/V/F. The background title is set in the downloaded copy. The Windows helper offers to copy the active roast title; paste into Roast → Properties before roasting. Background loading cannot set the active title. Keep Artisan’s date/time or batch counter in autosave filenames to distinguish repeated titles. After the roast, save the completed foreground .alog, mark the portal batch Roasted, then use Upload finished roast on Today or the preparation page. Preview, choose a reference, confirm and attach for curve review and tasting notes. Uploads do not overwrite production measurements or stock. The helper never starts or operates the roaster. Validate all alarms and USB behavior on your actual equipment before production use.

Hours and pay

Clock in and out, record breaks, and correct forgotten times with an audit reason. Administrators may grant a pay manager effective-dated rate access through PayrollAccess. Employees can view and export their own sheets. A payment marks hours through the chosen date paid. Estimates are straight time before deductions, not a payroll tax calculation.

Tools and maintenance

Optionally attach cooling-bed photos; camera use is not required. Record packaging supplies and flavoring rates to check demand. Record maintenance per machine; reminders use dates or recorded batches. Forecasts use actual green movements and current recipes, and display missing mappings instead of inventing usage.

Security and recovery

Use your configured HTTPS origin. Sign-in limits and CSRF protect saves; never expose the application port directly around the supplied proxy. Use unique accounts and strong passwords. Back up the database and instance secret together: changing that secret makes saved Shopify credentials unreadable. Exports contain private business data. See docs/BACKUPS.md and SECURITY.md.

How do I combine drafts after using Split?

On Plans, open Combine / rebalance drafts. Select at least two matching unstarted loads, choose a roaster, and preview the replacement. The selected green total stays the same; the preview shows the fewest balanced loads that fit its capacity. Unselected weights and each order’s labels, grind and bag sizes are preserved.

Save only after reviewing the preview. Original drafts remain in history; the result is manually sized and an accepted plan returns to Draft. Review and accept it again. Different coffees, roasts, finish temperatures or loss settings stay separate. Recorded production, used stock, started packing and green-origin requests/approvals cannot be combined here. The tool never starts a roaster.

Reset to automatic suggestions instead recalculates the whole coffee group from its current demand and stock. Use Combine when you want to preserve selected green weights and leave other loads as saved.

Machine minimums and small orders

Administration → Roasters stores each machine’s minimum and maximum green-bean input. Planning shows those ranges. Automatic suggestions may add green coffee to meet a minimum; the projected surplus is shown before acceptance. They create no batch when bulk already covers the target.

Manual saves, splitting, combining, acceptance and Artisan preparation check the same bounds. Splitting cannot create undersized loads. Combining preserves its selected total and refuses an impossible minimum instead of silently increasing it. Smaller orders can be covered by a minimum-sized roast with the excess visible in planning. After packing, weigh the leftover whole beans and record a new measured stock entry on Bulk roasted stock. Use only the actual leftover finished ounces, not the green minimum or its estimated yield. The full stock form accepts occasional standard-roast single origins while the regular quick-count list stays unchanged. No stock is credited automatically by planning or entering a roast result. Recorded physical roasts and workbook history retain their original measurements.
